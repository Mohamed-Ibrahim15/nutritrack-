Exercises

Fat-loss friendly ideas with muscle retention: steady cardio, strength, and flexible options for your level—spread across the week and keep sessions evenly spaced.

Walking & easy cardio

30–45 minutes brisk walking most days; low impact and pairs well with a calorie deficit. Aim for talk-but-not-sing pace.

Beginner friendly

Running & heart rate

2–3 easy-to-moderate sessions per week; add minutes gradually. Builds burn while keeping effort controlled.

Cardio

Indoor or outdoor cycling

40–60 minutes easy or moderate; kind to knees with good bike fit. Split AM/PM if that fits your schedule.

Low impact

Swimming

30–45 minutes light or moderate swim; joint-friendly with solid calorie burn. 2–3× weekly when you have pool access.

Full body

Full-body strength

Squat or leg press, row, push or bench, hinge, core. 2–3×8–12 reps, ~48h before repeating the same pattern.

Muscle retention

HIIT (optional)

Short intervals e.g. 30s hard / 60s easy, 1–2× weekly if joints tolerate it. Consistency beats random spikes.

Time efficient

Lower-body focus

Goblet squat 3×10–12, step-ups 3×8 each leg, glute bridge 3×12–15. Good form protects knees and supports weekly burn.

Legs & glutes

Upper, core & cardio finisher

DB row 3×8–12, push-up or incline 3×6–15, plank 3×30–60s; then incline walk 15–20 min or jump rope 5×1 min.

Safety

If you have heart disease, uncontrolled blood pressure, diabetes complications, or joint pain, get clearance from a clinician before increasing intensity. Hydrate, warm up, and progress gradually.
